INDEPENDENT SAILMAKING · LATVIA

MADE IN LATVIA. MADE FOR THE SEA.

Every sail begins
with a careful hand.

Based in Latvia, this independent sailmaking practice specialises in the individual production of sails for yachts, boats and ships.

Each sail is made personally by the sailmaker, bringing a hands-on approach to every stage of its creation. The focus is simple: thoughtful workmanship and a sail made for its purpose.
